## 💡 Tag Strategy Tips

### Primary Focus
- Lead with technology (react, dashboard, admin)
- Highlight key features (responsive, dark-mode, charts)
- Emphasize quality (professional, modern, premium)

### Secondary Focus
- Target specific use cases (saas, crm, analytics)
- Highlight technical benefits (tailwind, vite, mobile-first)
- Appeal to quality seekers (clean, elegant, enterprise)

### Avoid
- Generic terms (template, website, design)
- Overly technical terms (jsx, hooks, context)
- Redundant terms (responsive + mobile-responsive)
